About Treasury

A Digital Treasury Built on Stellar

Treasury is designed as a digital infrastructure project for tokenized treasury-style assets. Its assets are issued on the Stellar blockchain and can be independently inspected through public blockchain data.


The project combines a dedicated issuer account, treasury asset management and an informational platform designed to make on-chain assets easier to understand and explore.

Treasury History

From Public Finance to Digital Assets

The United States Treasury Department was established in 1789. Alexander Hamilton became its first Secretary and helped establish the foundations of the country's federal financial system. Treasury's history includes the development of public credit, financial administration and the construction of the historic Treasury Building in Washington, D.C.
